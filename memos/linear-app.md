# Linear

Purpose-built for planning and building products with AI agents.

https://linear.app

## Verdict: Watch

Linear presents a compelling growth story with exceptional team pedigree (Airbnb/Uber alumni), strong product-market fit evidenced by 40,000+ teams and $100M ARR, and impressive competitive momentum (67% YoY customer gains vs Jira's 32% decline). However, the 12.5-13x revenue multiple at $1.25B valuation demands sustained hyper-growth that faces headwinds from their narrow engineering-team focus and Jira's enterprise entrenchment. The score of 67.9 falls in the 'Watch' range, and while fundamentals are strong, the risk-reward at current valuation merits monitoring for another funding cycle or clearer cross-functional expansion before committing capital.

### What might change your mind

- Linear's AI agent positioning and Coding Sessions feature could unlock a significantly larger TAM beyond traditional project management into the AI-native development workflow market, justifying premium valuation multiples similar to other AI-first tools
- The net-negative lifetime burn and product-led growth model suggests Linear could achieve profitability at scale without additional dilution, making the current 13x multiple more reasonable if they sustain 280% profit growth rates
- Developer-led bottom-up adoption creating organizational 'revolts' when switching away indicates exceptionally strong product moat and expansion potential—once Linear captures engineering, they may naturally expand to adjacent functions rather than needing explicit cross-functional features
- Jira's 32% customer decline suggests a generational platform shift is underway; Linear's timing with remote-first teams and AI workflows positions them to capture the majority of net-new engineering organizations over the next decade

### Team

The founders previously sold a Y Combinator startup. The team includes veterans from Uber, Airbnb, and Coinbase. The company has grown from fewer than 30 employees in late 2020 to 271 employees as of June 2026. Founders started Linear from frustrations building software projects at high-growth companies, demonstrating strong founder-market fit.

### Product

Linear is an issue management and product development platform for software teams, launched in 2019 and now serving over 40,000 product teams including OpenAI, Cash App, and Ramp. Built on a proprietary Sync Engine for offline-first, instantaneous interactions, the platform integrates deeply with GitHub, Figma, Slack, and 1000+ tools via Zapier. Known for beautiful, fast interface and keyboard shortcuts (the 'anti-Jira'), Linear recently added AI-powered agents for workflows, triaging, PRs, and code review, including Coding Sessions feature. The product is opinionated, focused on simplicity and speed for engineering teams, with a developer-first design philosophy.

- Powers over 40,000 product teams including OpenAI, Cash App, and Ramp ([source](https://linear.app/about), high)
- Runs on Linear's proprietary Sync Engine, an offline-first data layer that makes every interaction feel instantaneous ([source](https://golden.com/wiki/Linear_(company)-R9DXJNW), high)
- Integrates with GitHub, GitLab, Slack, Figma, and over 1000 other tools with Zapier ([source](https://golden.com/wiki/Linear_(company)-R9DXJNW), high)
- AI-powered agents for workflows, triaging issues, creating PRs, code review, and Coding Sessions feature ([source](https://linear.app/about), high)
- Best known for beautiful, fast interface and keyboard shortcuts; feels like the anti-Jira ([source](https://www.spyglassci.com/blog/why-linear-won-project-management), high)
- Serves over 15,000 companies including OpenAI, Scale AI, and Perplexity ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/10/atlassian-rival-linear-raises-82m-at-1-25b-valuation/), high)

### Market

Linear operates in the $10B+ project management software market, specifically targeting the developer project management segment ($12B+). The market has three tiers: legacy incumbents (Jira with 300,000+ organizations), general-purpose tools (Asana, Monday, ClickUp), and developer-first challengers (Linear, Shortcut, Plane.so). Linear is gaining significant traction: won 1,351 new companies Feb-Apr 2026 vs Jira's 1,524 (67% YoY gain for Linear vs 32% drop for Jira), grew profits 280% in 2024, and reached $100M ARR in 2025 (up from $8.4M in 2023). The timing is strong with AI-era positioning for agent-based workflows.

### Risk

Linear raised $134M total at $1.25B-$1.3B valuation (June 2025) on $100M ARR, implying 12.5-13x revenue multiple requiring sustained hyper-growth. Despite strong metrics (net-negative lifetime burn, product-led growth, deep integrations making it sticky), key risks include: (1) narrow focus on engineering teams limits cross-functional adoption—marketing/sales teams need separate tools, (2) Jira's deep enterprise entrenchment with high switching costs, (3) opinionated product design creates limitations for complex enterprise workflows. However, Linear shows strong organic expansion with teams 'revolting' when management tries to switch tools, and continues winning greenfield engineering teams and developer-led decisions.

- Raised $82 million Series C led by Accel at $1.25 billion valuation, total raised $134.2 million (June 2025) ([source](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/10/atlassian-rival-linear-raises-82m-at-1-25b-valuation/), high)
- Net-negative lifetime burn rate — they've basically never spent the money they've raised ([source](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/linear), high)
- Jira will continue to dominate enterprise IT and legacy engineering organizations where switching cost exceeds benefit of Linear's simplicity ([source](https://www.spyglassci.com/blog/why-linear-won-project-management), high)
- Linear is designed for engineering teams; marketing and sales teams will feel limited, usually requiring another project management tool ([source](https://www.spyglassci.com/blog/why-linear-won-project-management), high)
- There have been times where management requests to consolidate tooling and teams using Linear revolt; that happened within Sequoia itself ([source](https://sequoiacap.com/article/linear-spotlight), high)
- Product-led growth model keeps customer acquisition costs low while enabling rapid organic expansion ([source](https://www.spyglassci.com/blog/why-linear-won-project-management), high)
